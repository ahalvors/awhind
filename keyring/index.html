      What KeyRing checks

      01 · RENEWAL

      Domain registration

      Who your registrar is, when the name expires, and whether the owner is visible or privacy-protected.

      02 · DNS

      Your domain’s “phone book”

      Which company runs your nameservers, and whether leftover settings point two directions.

      03 · EMAIL

      Who handles your email

      Your email provider, read from the domain’s mail records.

      04 · SPOOFING

      SPF & DMARC

      Whether inboxes can tell real email from you apart from a scammer pretending to be you.

      05 · SECURITY

      Security certificate

      Whether your site loads securely, and when its certificate runs out.

      06 · UPTIME

      Website & host

      Whether the site is up right now, and which company appears to host it.
