Our Services

At AWH Industries, we deliver Flexible IT Services for Modern Workplaces, ensuring your business stays connected, secure, and productive with reliable support tailored to your unique needs.

Network Buildout

Network Buildouts from scratch are all about creating a fast, secure, and reliable infrastructure tailored to your office needs. Using trusted solutions like UniFi, we design and deploy everything-from cabling and Wi-Fi to firewalls and VLANs-ensuring top-tier performance and enterprise-grade security from day one. Perfect for small businesses that want peace of mind and room to grow.

IT Support

We set up Office 365 and Google Workspace to get your team up and running with email, calendars, cloud storage, and collaboration tools-fast and hassle-free. From initial setup to ongoing end-user support, we handle it all so your team stays productive, secure, and supported every step of the way.

Physical Security

Physical Security Systems like card readers and door entry solutions provide smart, secure access control for your office. Integrated with your company directory, they streamline employee access while allowing controlled guest entry-keeping your space safe, efficient, and fully auditable.
