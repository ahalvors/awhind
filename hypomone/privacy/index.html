Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Hypomone is a workout planning app published by Andy Halvorsen ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Hypomone iPhone app or website.

The short version

We do not collect any data. Hypomone has no accounts, no servers that receive your information, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your training profile (goal, location, experience, schedule), workout history, sets, weights, body measurements, nutrition inputs, XP and reminder settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, or payment details. The app asks Apple only whether your Apple ID has an active Hypomone subscription.

Notifications

If you turn on workout reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is used.

Children

Hypomone does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Health information

Hypomone does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Changes

If this changes (for example, if we add optional sync), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before release.
