Privacy policy

What we collect when you use awhind.com, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Who we are

This site, awhind.com, is operated by AWH Industries (“AWHIND,” “we,” “us”). Questions about this policy can be sent to info@awhind.com.

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on this site (for example a security check, an assessment request, or a contact form) or email us, we receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, business or organization name, and the details you type in.

We use that information to respond to you, deliver what you asked for, and follow up about related services. We do not sell your personal information.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-25EMLK6VCM) to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, how you arrived at the site, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

The site is hosted on Netlify, which, like most web hosts, processes technical request data such as IP address, browser user agent, and the pages requested in order to serve and secure the site.

Service providers

We rely on service providers to run this site, including Netlify (hosting and site functions) and Google (analytics). They receive information only as needed to provide their services to us.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on. The site still works if you do.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. See our affiliate disclosure for details. When you follow a link to another site, that site’s privacy policy applies.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or delete it. To make a request, email info@awhind.com.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
